Shelf Pulse handover

What changed from the old dashboard, and why

This page compares the original mockup (shelf_pulse_ui_mockup.html) with the new dashboard on this canvas. It covers each section, each number and how it's calculated, the wording, and the design measurements, so the team can build it without guessing.

#
Open the dashboard on the Claude Design canvas and press Show changes in its top bar. Every changed area gets a purple numbered marker, and the numbers match the # column in section 2. Markers 15 to 19 appear on the store panel and the other pages, so click a store or switch pages to see them.
